What a section is A section is a small registered unit — an id, a title, an icon, and its rows. The shell knows how to lay out a row (label, description, control, revert) and nothing else. Adding a key to brink.toml means adding a row to a section, or a section to the list; it never means editing this page. That is what keeps the form from drifting behind the parser, which is the failure mode a hand-built form has.
