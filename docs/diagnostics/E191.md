# E191: inline alternatives on one line enumerate to more whole-line variants than the cap allows

A content line whose inline stateful alternatives are all textual compiles
into one enumerated **variant group** (#3274, the shared-alternatives
track): every combination of the alternatives' branches becomes a real
whole-line entry in the line table — a translation unit and a VO slot.
That product is therefore bounded (`VARIANT_CAP` in
`lir::lower::recognize`, currently 32): `{a|b|c|d} {w|x|y|z} {p|q}` is 32
variants and compiles; add one more branch anywhere and the product
exceeds the cap.

The breach is a hard error rather than a silent fallback, deliberately: a
line that quietly stopped being VO-addressable — its variants no longer
individual line entries — would change what translators and voice scripts
see with no visible signal. Split the line, or move one alternative onto
its own line, to bring the product back under the cap.
