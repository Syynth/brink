The Player draws a speaker in the colour `brink.toml`'s `[cast]` gives them

`[cast.Mara]` with `color = "#d97757"` colours Mara's cue, rule and spine
segment, in the Player and in the Conventions preview; the name matches a
cue ignoring case, so it covers a screenplay's `MARA`. A speaker the cast
does not list keeps the automatic palette colour.

A new **Settings ▸ Project ▸ Cast** section edits the table: a colour well
and a remove button per speaker, and a row that adds one. Edits keep the
file's comments and key order, and `cast` is now a reserved settings
section id.
