// The Toppled Temple — a one-delve dungeon for the brink studio playground.
// You are an unnamed delver. The only voices down here belong to a sardonic
// merchant, a riddling ghost, a nervous goblin, and twelve tons of stone.
//
// This story leans on the fuller ink toolbox on purpose — LISTs, functions,
// sequences, and a thread — so it doubles as a workout for the compiler.

VAR gold = 12
VAR health = 10
VAR torch = 6
VAR golem_hp = 12
VAR rounds = 0
VAR weakness_known = false

LIST Gear = sword, shield, charm, draught, sparetorch
VAR pack = ()

LIST Boon = blessed, cursed
VAR boon = ()

LIST Rooms = riddle, altar, armory
VAR explored = ()

// ── helpers ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

=== function carrying(item) ===
~ return pack ? item

=== function roll(lo, hi) ===
~ return RANDOM(lo, hi)

=== function spend_torch(n) ===
~ torch = torch - n
{ torch < 0:
    ~ torch = 0
}

=== function torch_desc() ===
{
    - torch <= 0: has guttered out — you fight blind
    - torch <= 2: burns low, shadows crowding in
    - else: holds steady
}

=== function damage() ===
~ temp d = roll(2, 4)
{ carrying(sword):
    ~ d = d + 2
}
{ weakness_known:
    ~ d = d + 3
}
{ boon == blessed:
    ~ d = d + 1
}
{ boon == cursed:
    ~ d = d - 1
}
{ torch <= 0:
    ~ d = d - 1
}
{ d < 1:
    ~ d = 1
}
~ return d

// ── intro ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

=== intro ===
You descend the worn steps into the Toppled Temple. Somewhere below, water drips with the patience of the very old.

Your torch is lit. You have {gold} coin and a stubborn disposition.

A figure unfolds from the shadow of a broken pillar — all patched cloak and one glinting eye.

@GRISWOLD:<>
(not getting up)<>
Buying or dying? I haven't got all century, and neither, frankly, have you.

* [Browse his wares]
    -> barter
* [Push past him]
    He shrugs, a man long past being insulted by the doomed.
    -> threshold

// ── barter (the shop loop) ──────────────────────────────────────────

=== barter ===
Griswold spreads a stained cloth. On it: the usual last-chance assortment.
- (opts)
You have {gold} coin.
+ {gold >= 6 and not carrying(sword)} [A short sword — 6 coin]
    ~ gold = gold - 6
    ~ pack += sword
    @GRISWOLD:<>
    Pointy end goes in the monster. Try to remember which end.
    -> opts
+ {gold >= 5 and not carrying(shield)} [A dented shield — 5 coin]
    ~ gold = gold - 5
    ~ pack += shield
    -> opts
+ {gold >= 7 and not carrying(charm)} [A warding charm — 7 coin]
    ~ gold = gold - 7
    ~ pack += charm
    @GRISWOLD:<>
    (lowering his voice)<>
    Old temple silver. Stone doesn't care for it. Don't ask how I know.
    -> opts
+ {gold >= 3 and not carrying(draught)} [A healing draught — 3 coin]
    ~ gold = gold - 3
    ~ pack += draught
    -> opts
+ {gold >= 2 and not carrying(sparetorch)} [A spare torch — 2 coin]
    ~ gold = gold - 2
    ~ pack += sparetorch
    -> opts
* [Enough shopping]
    @GRISWOLD:<>
    (already counting your coin)<>
    Pleasure doing business. Do scream if you need me. I won't come.
    -> threshold

// ── threshold (the hub) ─────────────────────────────────────────────

=== threshold ===
{ explored == ():
    The temple opens into a toppled nave. Three ways lead off into the dark.
- else:
    You stand again in the toppled nave.
}
Your torch {torch_desc()}.

* {not (explored ? riddle)} [The whispering hall]
    -> riddle_hall
* {not (explored ? altar)} [The cracked altar]
    -> cursed_altar
* {not (explored ? armory)} [The collapsed armory]
    -> armory
* {explored != ()} [Approach the vault doors]
    -> vault_door

=== riddle_hall ===
~ explored += riddle
~ spend_torch(1)
A corridor of weeping stone. A pale shape gathers at its end — a SPECTRE, thin as candle smoke.

@SPECTRE:<>
(a voice like a draft under a door)<>
Answer me true and I'll name the Warden's flaw. Answer false and... well. I'll laugh.

"I have no throat, yet I swallow your light. I have no hands, yet I hold the whole temple. What am I?"

* ["The dark"]
    @SPECTRE:<>
    (delighted)<>
    Just so. And the Warden is a thing of the dark — so bring it light. Strike while your torch burns bright; it flinches from the flame.
    ~ weakness_known = true
    -> threshold
* ["The Warden"]
    @SPECTRE:<>
    Flattering. Wrong.
    -> threshold
* ["Your mother"]
    @SPECTRE:<>
    (after a long pause)<>
    ...I am three centuries dead and that still landed. Wrong, though.
    -> threshold

=== cursed_altar ===
~ explored += altar
~ spend_torch(1)
A black altar, split down the middle. Old offerings rot in the crack — and something gleams beneath them.

* [Kneel and pray]
    A cold blessing settles over you like a clean sheet. Your wounds knit a little.
    ~ boon = blessed
    ~ health = health + 2
    -> threshold
* [Pocket the gleaming thing]
    Twelve coin of temple gold. The altar exhales, and a chill follows you out and does not leave.
    ~ gold = gold + 12
    ~ boon = cursed
    -> threshold
* [Leave it well alone]
    Wise. Rare, but wise.
    -> threshold

=== armory ===
~ explored += armory
~ spend_torch(1)
The armory roof gave out an age ago. Rusted racks, a few salvageable blades — and a goblin perched on the best of them, gnawing something you decline to identify.

@GRIK:<>
(scrambling upright)<>
{~Mine! Found it first!|Don't — don't look at me like that!|I'm very dangerous! Ask anyone! ...there's no one to ask.}

* {carrying(sword) or carrying(shield)} [Advance, armed]
    You take one deliberate step. Grik reconsiders his entire career.
    @GRIK:<>
    (fleeing)<>
    Keep the rack! Keep the temple! I never liked it here!
    { carrying(sword):
        ~ pack += shield
    - else:
        ~ pack += sword
    }
    You help yourself to what he abandoned.
    -> threshold
* {gold >= 3} [Bribe him — 3 coin]
    ~ gold = gold - 3
    Grik bites the coin, beams, and presses a surprisingly decent blade into your hands.
    ~ pack += sword
    -> threshold
* [Snuff your torch and slip past]
    ~ spend_torch(2)
    Slow work in the dark, but you pocket a spare from the rack as you go.
    ~ pack += sparetorch
    -> threshold

=== vault_door ===
Two vault doors, each three men tall, hang half off their hinges. Beyond: a chamber, and in it something enormous and patient.

{weakness_known: You remember the Spectre — strike while your torch burns bright.}
{not weakness_known: You have the distinct sense you forgot to ask someone something important.}

You step through. The doors groan shut behind you, because of course they do.
-> warden_golem

// ── the Warden (battle loop) ────────────────────────────────────────

=== warden_golem ===
{ golem_hp <= 0: -> victory }
{ health <= 0: -> defeat }
~ rounds = rounds + 1

{!The Warden rises — a hill of fitted stone, eyes like coals in a cold grate. It does not hurry. It has never had to.}

The Warden {~grinds a step closer|considers you, slow as weather|rolls its shoulders with the sound of a rockslide}.
{
    - golem_hp > 8: It stands entirely unbothered.
    - golem_hp > 4: A crack runs up its flank now.
    - else: It lists badly, one arm hanging.
}
Your torch {torch_desc()}.{health <= 3:  You are badly hurt.}

<- specials
+ [Strike]
    ~ temp d = damage()
    ~ golem_hp = golem_hp - d
    You strike for {d}.{weakness_known and torch > 2:  The flame flares as you swing; the Warden flinches.}
    -> warden_turn
+ {carrying(draught)} [Drink the draught (+5)]
    ~ pack -= draught
    ~ health = health + 5
    The draught burns going down. Better.
    -> warden_turn
* {torch <= 0 and carrying(sparetorch)} [Light your spare torch]
    ~ pack -= sparetorch
    ~ torch = 6
    Light floods back, and the Warden actually recoils.
    -> warden_turn

= specials
+ {carrying(charm) and golem_hp > 0} [Crush the warding charm against it]
    ~ pack -= charm
    Temple silver hisses against stone; the Warden sloughs a slab of its hide like old skin.
    ~ golem_hp = golem_hp - 5
    -> warden_turn
- -> DONE

= warden_turn
{ golem_hp <= 0: -> warden_golem }
The Warden answers — {~a backhand like a falling wall|a stamp that splits the floor|a slow, grinding grab}.
~ temp hit = roll(1, 3)
{ carrying(shield):
    ~ hit = hit - 1
}
{ boon == cursed:
    ~ hit = hit + 1
}
{ hit < 0:
    ~ hit = 0
}
~ health = health - hit
{hit <= 0: You turn it aside.|It catches you for {hit}.}
~ spend_torch(1)
-> warden_golem

// ── endings ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

=== victory ===
The Warden comes apart at the seams — a slow avalanche of dressed stone, then stillness, then dust.

The vault behind it stands open. Inside: enough old temple gold to make the whole miserable trip worthwhile.
~ gold = gold + 40

You climb back toward daylight. Griswold is exactly where you left him.

@GRISWOLD:<>
(eyeing your bulging pack)<>
Well, well. The corpse walks, and rich besides. Tell you what — I'll take that gold off your hands at a generous rate.

* [Haggle him to a standstill]
    @GRISWOLD:<>
    (grinning)<>
    There it is. Knew I liked you.
    You step out into the light {gold} coin heavier and only slightly cursed.
    -> END
* [Walk past him into the sun]
    You do not even slow down.
    @GRISWOLD:<>
    (calling after)<>
    Sensible! Do come back when you're greedy again!
    -> END

=== defeat ===
The last blow folds you against the cold floor. The Warden settles back into its patient waiting, as if you were never more than a draft.

Your torch goes out. Then the dark takes the rest.
-> END
